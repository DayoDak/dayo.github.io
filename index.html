Intro

Numbers don't lie but they don't always speak clearly either. That's where I come in. I'm Adedayo Kofoworade, a Data & Financial Analyst with 4+ years of experience helping organisations in technology, government, and agriculture make sharper, faster decisions.

I specialise in two things that rarely come in the same person: the technical depth to wrangle and model complex data, and the financial fluency to interpret what it means for the bottom line.

From building revenue dashboards for a state government agency to developing financial models that drove 10% cost savings for a tech company, my work is grounded in real outcomes not just pretty charts.

I work across Python, R, SQL, Tableau, Excel, and Google Cloud, and I'm the kind of analyst who won't hand you a report without telling you what to do with it.

Consumer Price Index and
Exchange Rate Trends
in Nigeria.

Nigeria's currency has experienced dramatic swings over the decades — but how closely does inflation follow? This Tableau project investigates the relationship between the Consumer Price Index (CPI) and the Dollar–Naira exchange rate from 1995 to 2021, revealing a non-linear dynamic that defies simple assumptions. The dashboard enables users to explore how devaluation events correspond with inflation spikes, offering useful context for economic policy analysis and investment planning. Learn more

Reported Measle Cases.

Despite being a vaccine-preventable disease, measles remains a public health threat across many countries. This SQL/Tableau project analyses reported cases across all 194 WHO member countries from 2011 to 2022, identifying which regions carry the highest burden and how outbreak patterns have shifted over time. The goal: surface data-driven insights that can inform immunisation prioritisation and public health resource allocation. Learn more

Mass Shooting in USA.

Gun violence in America follows patterns — geographic, seasonal, and demographic — that aggregate statistics often obscure. Using a dataset of 2,356 incidents spanning 2018 to 2022, this SQL and Tableau project maps hotspots, identifies trends over time, and highlights concentrations by state and city. The analysis provides a data-driven lens on a deeply complex issue, demonstrating how structured querying and visual storytelling can make large datasets accessible and meaningful. Learn more

Twitter Text Analysis.

What were Nigerians actually saying about their presidential candidates on social media? This RStudio project applied Natural Language Processing (NLP) to over 9,000 tweets across the three major political parties, tracking how public sentiment shifted in the weeks leading up to the 2023 election. The analysis reveals not just who people supported, but how the tone of conversation evolved — a reminder that the real story in data is often about change over time. Learn more
